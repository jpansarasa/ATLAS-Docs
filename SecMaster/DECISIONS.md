# SecMaster — DECISIONS detail

The DECISIONS block of `AGENT_README.md` carries one line per decision: the RULE, its
PRECOND, and the GUARD/TEST citations. This file carries the EVIDENCE behind each rule --
the measurement that established it, the alternatives rejected, and the traps that make the
rule non-obvious. One `## D-n <slug>` section per entry; the card points here per entry.

READ THE CARD FIRST. Open a section here only when you are about to change the guard it
describes, or when the card's rule line is not enough to tell you WHY the rule is what it
is. Nothing here overrides the card -- where the two disagree, the card is the current rule
and the disagreement is a defect to report (CLAUDE.md INTENT_FIDELITY CONFLICT).
